# Upstream model source

- Repository: https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl
- Revision: `0daab160d3fb1c2cbaba16a4a19052f36709dd9e` (branch `develop`, retrieved 2026-11-14)
- Robot model: `src/mjlab_microduck/robot/microduck/robot_groundcontact.xml`
- Meshes: the 38 STL files referenced by that MJCF from `src/mjlab_microduck/robot/microduck/assets/`
- The MJCF and mesh files are copied without modification. `scene_head_stabilization.xml` adapts the upstream scene wrapper to include `robot_groundcontact.xml`; it retains the upstream STAND keyframe and adds no robot geometry.
- `head_neck_rig.xml` is generated by `scripts/build_head_rig.py` from the copied MJCF: it retains the trunk and original four-joint head/neck chain, removes both leg branches and their actuators, and makes the trunk a MuJoCo mocap body so its motion is prescribed for the isolated gaze-stabilization experiment.
